# Token logos

Unmodified PNG assets from [Alcor UI](https://github.com/alcorexchange/alcor-ui/tree/e4f382eb2979c2a2816474e2f830d7e9f4e05ec1/assets/tokens/wax), retrieved 2026-09-28.

Alcor's [token logo documentation](https://docs.alcor.exchange/adding-token-information/token-logo) specifies `assets/tokens/<chain>/<symbol>_<contract>.png`, using lowercase names and 64×64 PNGs.

Pinned source commit: `e4f382eb2979c2a2816474e2f830d7e9f4e05ec1`.

Each logo is mapped to its exact symbol, contract, and precision in `src/services/tokenMetadata.js`. Assets are bundled locally for reliable delivery. Token artwork and branding belong to their respective projects.

## CleanupCentr descriptions

These descriptions are published in CleanupCentr's Exchange, not submitted to Alcor.

- **CINDER — Burn rewards & energy:** Turn NFT cleanup into your next action. Earn CINDER by burning approved NFTs through Incinerators, then spend it to recharge personal and farm energy.
- **TRASH — Incinerator fuel:** Keep the cleanup running. TRASH fuels Incinerators in the Burn Center, supporting the process that burns approved NFTs and produces CINDER.
- **TOMATOE — Harvests & farm supplies:** Put your harvest back to work. TOMATOE is a farming resource earned from configured crop rewards and used for eligible shop items and blend recipes.
- **BANANAZ — Blend ingredient:** Build your next farming supply. BANANAZ is used in supported blend recipes, including EcoFusion Compost, alongside the recipe’s other required tokens and NFTs.
